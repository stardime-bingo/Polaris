Polaris adapts the expanding inset stroke from PulseStrokeAnimationModifier
into CompletionPulse in Docket/Views/ConfettiView.swift. It uses one local
18–34 pt circle, a bounded easing curve, a 600 ms duration, and cancellable
Swift concurrency for its lifetime. Pow's generic particle layers, timer
queue, blur and brightness adjustments are not included. No Pow package
dependency is installed. The completion check and UndoToast are Polaris code.
